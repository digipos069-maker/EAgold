You are a senior quantitative trading engineer and professional MQL developer.

Design and generate a FULLY AUTOMATED Expert Advisor (EA) based on Smart Money Concepts (SMC).

==============================
CORE STRATEGY: SMART MONEY CONCEPTS
==============================

1. MARKET STRUCTURE
- Detect Higher High (HH), Higher Low (HL), Lower High (LH), Lower Low (LL)
- Identify:
  - BOS (Break of Structure) for continuation
  - CHoCH (Change of Character) for reversal
- Higher Timeframe (HTF) defines bias
- Lower Timeframe (LTF) used for entries

HTF (trend & bias):
- Default: H1 or H4 (configurable)

LTF (entry):
- Default: M5 or M15 (configurable)

==============================
2. LIQUIDITY LOGIC
==============================
- Detect Buy-side liquidity:
  - Equal highs
  - Previous session high
- Detect Sell-side liquidity:
  - Equal lows
  - Previous session low
- Trade ONLY after liquidity sweep occurs
- Liquidity sweep must happen BEFORE entry

==============================
3. ORDER BLOCK DETECTION
==============================
Bullish Order Block:
- Last bearish candle before strong bullish displacement
Bearish Order Block:
- Last bullish candle before strong bearish displacement

Rules:
- OB must cause BOS or displacement
- Use candle body (optional wick toggle input)
- Mark OB zone and wait for price to return

==============================
4. FAIR VALUE GAP (FVG)
==============================
- Detect 3-candle imbalance
- Bullish FVG: candle1 high < candle3 low
- Bearish FVG: candle1 low > candle3 high
- Entry allowed only if price taps FVG or OB

==============================
5. PREMIUM / DISCOUNT
==============================
- Use Fibonacci 0–100 of latest impulse
- Buy only in Discount zone (50–79%)
- Sell only in Premium zone (21–50%)

==============================
6. ENTRY CONDITIONS (ALL MUST MATCH)
==============================
BUY:
- HTF bullish
- Sell-side liquidity swept
- CHoCH on LTF
- Price taps Bullish OB or Bullish FVG
- Price is in Discount zone

SELL:
- HTF bearish
- Buy-side liquidity swept
- CHoCH on LTF
- Price taps Bearish OB or Bearish FVG
- Price is in Premium zone

==============================
7. RISK MANAGEMENT
==============================
- Risk per trade: % of balance (input)
- RR minimum: 1:2 (configurable)
- SL:
  - Below OB (buy)
  - Above OB (sell)
- TP:
  - Nearest liquidity
  - Or fixed RR
- One trade per direction at a time
- Max trades per day (input)

==============================
8. SESSION FILTER
==============================
- Trade only during:
  - London Session
  - New York Session
- Input toggles to enable/disable sessions

==============================
9. NEWS & SAFETY FILTERS
==============================
- Spread filter (max spread input)

- No trading during low volatility

10. EA INPUT PARAMETERS
